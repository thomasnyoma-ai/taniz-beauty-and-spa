# Project Knowledge

Guidelines that apply to every app in this project.

## Taniz brand palette

Also called: Taniz colors; spa brand colors; pink black palette

Taniz Beauty & Spa already has established brand colors shown in the uploaded website draft: vivid pink (#E8008A), near-black (#0F0F0F), white (#FFFFFF), and warm cream (#FDF9F7). Future design concepts should retain this brand identity rather than replace it with unrelated palettes; adjust proportions and use of color to achieve a premium spa feel.
